While still formally a **Lab Tech**, I was already fully embedded in the **BPI Continuous Improvement and Systems team** when I completed the **PRINCE2 Foundation** course in October 2023, along with many cross-departmental colleagues.

> ✅ Passed the official exam on **25 October 2023**  
> 🔄 **Renewal planned before 2026** to maintain certification status and relevance

This was my first formal exposure to **structured project management**, having previously contributed in technical or assistant roles rather than leading or overseeing delivery.

The course introduced the value of:
- **Defined roles and responsibilities**, with a clear separation between governance (**Project Board**) and execution (**Project Team**)
- **Stage-based control**, allowing structured checkpoints and risk management to control flow and continued justification
- **Product-based planning**, where deliverables are agreed upfront and measured against quality criteria
- Emphasis on **continued business justification**, accountability, and lessons learned (either from mistakes or successes)

It helped formalise much of the instinctive project support I'd been doing, giving me the tools to actively contribute to planning, risk tracking, and stakeholder management within improvement initiatives and as I began managing my own projects.
