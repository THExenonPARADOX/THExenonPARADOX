# 🛠️ Lean Six Sigma – Black Belt Certified

I undertook the **Lean Six Sigma Black Belt (LSSBB)** certification entirely off my own initiative and funding, driven by a desire to go beyond the standard Green Belt path.  
Originally planning to wait, I was invited to an elite online class taught by one of **tKA’s top Master Black Belts** — known for a **100% pass rate** with consistently high-scoring candidates.

> ✅ **Certified Black Belt** in Lean Six Sigma  
> 🧠 **Achieved a 100% final score**

The course included both advanced improvement tools and **MBA-level strategic content**, such as:
- **Hoshin Kanri X-Matrix** – aligning improvement objectives with business strategy
- **DMAIC** and **DMADV** frameworks
- **Root Cause Analysis** (5 Whys, Fishbone Diagrams, FMEA)
- **Statistical Process Control (SPC)** and process capability metrics (Cp, Cpk)
- **Voice of the Customer (VOC)** and CTQ flow-down

The programme formalised my improvement knowledge and elevated my thinking to a more strategic level, integrating business alignment with technical process control.
