# 🧪 From Admin to Analyst – My Professional Journey

After earning my BSc in Mathematics & Physics, I entered the cosmetics industry through a **contract admin role** in September 2021.  
This was my first real experience in a commercial environment — and the first step on a path I hadn’t planned, but wouldn’t trade.

---

## 🔬 Into the Lab (Jan 2022)

In January 2022, I transitioned from admin into a **lab technician role**, supporting formulation testing, development batches, and assisting chemists during NPD.  
This was a varied, hands-on role, and many of the manual processes sparked my interest in streamlining and automation.

One example was a custom results `.xls` file I built to improve our testing documentation. However, because it hadn’t been signed off through the Quality Management System (QMS), it couldn't be used officially.  
As this was my first major employer, it was a valuable lesson in why **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)** and governance matter — and this moment became the springboard for my future in **Business Process Improvement (BPI)**.

---

## 🗃️ Into the Data (May 2022)

By May 2022, following that “unofficial” improvement attempt, I was moved into a **Database Administrator** role to support the Project Implementer (another PV admin and one of my early mentors).  
Around 20% of my time was spent supporting the rollout of a new **COSHH tool** in ProductVision, and I was eventually made responsible for writing and training the new COSHH SOP — my **informal entry into BPI**.

---

## 🛠️ From Contract to Core (Aug 2022)

In August 2022, I became a **permanent employee**, just as the company began restructuring its operational teams.  
My original Ops team was dissolved, and out of that emerged the **Continuous Improvement & Systems (CI&S)** team.

I took on a hybrid role (~80% focused on BPI), developing metric dashboards and predictive capacity models using **M (Power Query)**.  
I self-taught the language to improve SQL data extraction and reporting workflows, helping convert raw datasets into formal, visual outputs.  
Eventually, the lab support side of my role was phased out, and I became fully focused on BPI.

---

## 📈 Analyst Role (April 2024)

In April 2024, I was formally promoted from Lab Tech to **Assistant Analyst**.

I'm now involved in:
- Improving PLM and MRP systems (**ProductVision**, **QAD**)
- Workflow automation using **Modified VB**, **Power Query**, and **SQL**
- Authoring new SOPs and driving process standardisation
- Supporting project delivery using **PRINCE2** principles
- Leading global improvement initiatives using **Lean Six Sigma**, including **DMAIC** and **DMADV**

---

> I didn’t enter this field through a grad scheme or formal programme.  
> I started by solving problems that annoyed me, and I kept going.  
> What began with a clunky SOP in the lab became a full-time role in transformation and systems improvement.
